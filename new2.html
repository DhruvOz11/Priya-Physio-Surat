Meet Dr. Priya Nathani

Dr. Priya specializes in treating complex neurological conditions including stroke rehabilitation, Parkinson's disease, and multiple sclerosis. Her orthopedic expertise covers everything from sports injuries to post-surgical rehabilitation, while her cardio-pulmonary rehabilitation helps patients recover from heart surgeries and manage respiratory conditions.

As a Gujarat State Physiotherapy Council Registered professional and a Certified Pilates Instructor, she bridges the gap between medical rehabilitation and holistic fitness.
